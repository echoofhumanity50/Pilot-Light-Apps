Plain-language policy

Privacy

Pilot Light Apps collects only the limited information needed to understand whether the website is useful and working properly.

Website analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It provides aggregate information such as page views, referring websites, approximate country, browser, operating system, device type, and site-performance measurements.

We do not use this analytics setup to place advertising cookies, follow you across unrelated websites, or create a personal advertising profile. Cloudflare processes technical request information under its own privacy policy.

Wallpaper Pilot

Wallpaper Pilot operates on your Windows computer. It does not upload your wallpaper folders, favorites, dislikes, settings, or app-usage information to Pilot Light Apps. The app may contact GitHub only when you choose to check for an available update.

Display / Audio Switcher

Display / Audio Switcher operates on your Windows computer. It stores saved display and playback-device profiles locally and does not send those profiles or app-usage information to Pilot Light Apps. The app includes links that open the Pilot Light Apps website and donation page in your browser.

Downloads and outside services

Downloads are hosted by GitHub, which may record a release-asset download and process technical information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Donations are handled by Buy Me a Coffee under its own policies. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@pilotlightapps.com. Please do not email passwords, financial information, or confidential files.

Effective August 22, 2026. We will update this page if our data practices materially change.
